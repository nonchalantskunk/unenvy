Unenvy Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback? We'd like to hear from you.

Email: support@bodyai.us

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Unenvy has no accounts and no servers, so we cannot see your check-ins.

How do I delete my data?

Open the Settings tab and tap Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. If you back up your iPhone, copies may remain in those backups, which you control in your Apple settings.

Does Unenvy replace therapy?

No. It is a self-reflection tool, not therapy, medical advice, or a diagnosis. If envy or jealousy feels overwhelming or affects your sleep, work, or relationships, please talk to a therapist or counselor.

Emergency resources

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 (US). In the US you can also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. The Emergency Resources section of the Learn tab lists more free services.
