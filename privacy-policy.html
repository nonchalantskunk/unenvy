Unenvy Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Summary

Unenvy keeps everything on your device. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

What the app stores on your device

When you use Unenvy, it saves the check-ins you create: the type of feeling, the trigger category, any name or description you type, your intensity ratings, your notes, and the action you chose. It also saves your settings, such as your main trigger and whether you finished the intro. This information is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Unenvy has no servers and does not send your entries anywhere. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Apple and your device

If you turn on sharing of analytics or crash data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregate, anonymous reports. These do not include your entries or any personal information from the app.

Your control

You can delete a single check-in from History, or delete all of your data from Settings. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, copies of app data may be included in those backups. You control that in your Apple settings.

Children

Unenvy is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Unenvy is a self-reflection tool. It is not therapy, medical advice, or a diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for a professional. If envy or jealousy feels overwhelming or affects your sleep, work, or relationships, please talk to a therapist or counselor. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services. In the US, you can call or text 988.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled, such as syncing across devices, we will update this policy and the date above before those features are released.
